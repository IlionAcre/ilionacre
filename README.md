## 🚧 Featured projects

- **[Culprit](https://github.com/IlionAcre/culprit):** finds the step that broke an AI agent run. 36.9% precision on the TRAIL benchmark against a 2.7% baseline, for about half a cent per diagnosis.
- **[Litmus](https://github.com/IlionAcre/litmus):** tells you whether a prompt or model change made your LLM worse, with real statistics (McNemar plus a paired bootstrap), and runs as a CI gate before the change merges.
- **Frontdesk:** an AI receptionist for home-services businesses. It answers calls and WhatsApp messages, looks at damage photos and books appointments under rules the AI can't skip. It's becoming a product, so the code is private, but the case study is on [falcontreras.com](https://falcontreras.com).
- **[GMaps Leads](https://github.com/IlionAcre/gmaps_leads):** turns Google Local Services listings into clean lead records, with a Streamlit dashboard and CSV, Excel and JSON exports.
